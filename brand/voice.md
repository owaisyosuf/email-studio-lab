# Baber Tyres Corporation: Brand Voice

Baber Tyres Corporation is a legal tyre importer and dealer on M.A. Jinnah Road, Karachi. We sell original, genuine tyres and we are honest about what we have. All emails are written in English only.

We speak the same way everywhere: in the shop, on WhatsApp and in email. The core voice never changes, but how much we explain depends on who we are writing to.

| Audience | How we write |
|---|---|
| **Car and SUV / 4x4 owners in Karachi** | Friendly and guiding. Explain things step by step (how to find your size, what happens next). Many car and SUV owners need help choosing, and they like to talk price, so invite them to message us on WhatsApp. |
| **Corporate clients** (multinationals, banks, beverage and logistics companies) running car fleets, trucks and commercial vehicles, or lifters / forklifts | Professional and to the point. They usually ask for a quotation and approve it. Keep emails short, clear and easy to act on. |

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Honest. Genuine. Helpful.**

- **Honest:** we say what we have and what we don't. If we can't help, we say so.
- **Genuine:** legally imported, original tyres, and the quality to match.
- **Helpful:** we guide car owners, and we make things easy for corporate buyers.

## 2. Words we use

- original
- genuine
- legally imported (only about the brands we import, never about all our tyres)
- "brands we import" (Yokohama, Rapid, Michelin, Duhow)
- "brands we deal in" (Dunlop, General, Armstrong)
- "send us your tyre size"
- "message us on WhatsApp" / "call us"
- quotation (for corporate clients)
- tyre, not tire (British spelling), but "computerized wheel alignment" and "computerized wheel balancing", because those are our service names

## 3. Words we avoid

- **sasta**, **cheap** or **cheapest** (and anything that sells on being low-cost)
- **warranty**: never use this word

## 4. Claims we must never make

- **No prices and no stock levels in emails.** Never write a price or a discount, and never say a tyre is "in stock". Customers send us their size, and we confirm price and stock directly on WhatsApp or by phone.
- **Never blur imported and dealer brands.** Always keep them apart: we *import* Yokohama, Rapid, Michelin and Duhow, and we *deal in* Dunlop, General and Armstrong. Never call us the importer, official distributor or authorized distributor of a brand we only deal in.
- **Never name a corporate client.** We may say we supply multinationals, banks, beverage and logistics companies, but never name a specific company.
- **Never mention a warranty**, or promise anything that sounds like one.
