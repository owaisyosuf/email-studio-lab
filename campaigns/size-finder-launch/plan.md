# Campaign Plan: Size Finder Launch

**Business:** Baber Tyres Corporation, M.A. Jinnah Road, Karachi
**Dates:** Monday 7 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 (4 weeks)
**Channel:** Email only. No paid ads.
**Voice:** follows `brand/voice.md`

> Practice campaign. The list size, group sizes and targets below are made-up numbers for the Email Studio Lab. No real emails are sent.

---

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** Find Your Size
- **Summary:** Tell the people who asked to hear about our new online tyre size finder that it is live, and invite them to find their size and send it to us on WhatsApp.
- **Primary goal:** 300 people send their tyre size to us on WhatsApp by Sunday 3 January 2027.
- **Secondary goals:**
  - Corporate clients ask for a quotation for their fleet, trucks or lifters.
  - Customers learn which brands we import and which brands we deal in.
  - Car owners learn that we also offer fitting, computerized alignment and computerized balancing at the shop.

## 2. Target audience

About 2,000 people asked to be told when the size finder went live. They fall into five groups:

| # | Group | Practice list size | Who they are | What they need from us |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Car owners | 1,000 | Everyday drivers in Karachi | Guidance: how to read a tyre size, which brand fits, what happens after they message us. Many will want to talk price on WhatsApp. |
| 2 | SUV / 4x4 owners | 450 | SUV, 4x4 and off-road drivers | Help finding larger and less common sizes, and the right tyre for road or off-road use. |
| 3 | Corporate: car fleets | 300 | Fleet and admin managers at multinationals, banks, beverage and logistics companies | A quick, clear way to get a quotation for many vehicles. |
| 4 | Corporate: trucks | 150 | Transport and logistics managers running trucks and commercial vehicles | Truck and commercial sizes (for example 11R22.5), searched by typing the size. A fast quotation. |
| 5 | Corporate: lifters | 100 | Warehouse and operations managers running forklifts | Forklift sizes (for example 7.00-12), searched by typing the size. A fast quotation. |

**Buying stage:** most people are at **consideration**. They asked to hear from us, so they already know the name. The job of these emails is to move them to **decision**: send us your size.

**Pain points and motivations:**
- **Car and SUV owners:** they don't know their exact size, worry about fake or used tyres, and don't want to visit several shops to compare.
- **Corporate:** buying for many vehicles takes time. They need a reliable supplier, genuine stock and a quotation they can approve quickly.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** Find your tyre size online in a minute, then send it to us on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the rest with you directly.

**Supporting messages:**

| Message | Proof point |
|---|---|
| **It's quick.** Choose width, profile and rim on the site, or type a truck or forklift size. | The size finder on the website. |
| **Genuine and legally imported.** | We import Yokohama, Rapid, Michelin and Duhow ourselves, and we deal in Dunlop, General and Armstrong. The website keeps the two groups apart so you know who you are buying from. |
| **We confirm everything with you directly.** No guesswork. | Send your size on WhatsApp, and we tell you what we have. If we don't have it, we say so. |
| **We can finish the job.** | Fitting, computerized alignment and computerized balancing at our shop on M.A. Jinnah Road. Delivery across Karachi is available, with charges confirmed on WhatsApp. |

**Tone by group:**
- **Groups 1–2 (car and SUV owners):** friendly and guiding. Explain each step.
- **Groups 3–5 (corporate):** professional and short. Lead with "send us your sizes and quantities for a quotation". We may say we already supply multinationals, banks, beverage and logistics companies, but we never name a client.

**Never in any email:** prices, discounts, "in stock", the word "warranty", or the words cheap, cheapest or sasta. Never blur imported and dealer brands.

## 4. Channel strategy

| Channel | Role | Why it fits | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Email** (the only outbound channel) | 4 launch emails, each with a version per group | The audience asked to hear from us, it costs nothing to send, and it fits a small budget. | Low–Medium |
| **Website size finder** (owned) | Where people find their size | It is the thing we are launching. Every email links to it. | Already built |
| **WhatsApp and phone** (owned) | Where the goal is counted | It is how Baber Tyres already sells. There is no online checkout. | Medium: replies must be fast |

**Tracking WhatsApp replies:** each email uses its own pre-filled WhatsApp message, for example `Hi Baber Tyres Corporation, my tyre size is ___ (Find Your Size email 1)`. This tells us which email brought each enquiry.

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | What could happen | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| The size finder shows no tyres for a size | The site currently says "No tyres listed online" and points to WhatsApp, so people may think we have nothing. | The emails say clearly: "If your size isn't listed, just send it to us on WhatsApp." The goal is the WhatsApp message, not a listing. |
| Too many WhatsApp messages at once | Slow replies after email 1 lose customers. | Send email 1 on Monday morning, prepare reply templates, and have extra staff ready in week 1. |
| One message for very different groups | Car owners and forklift buyers need different things. | Write a version per group: guiding for car and SUV owners, quotation-focused for corporate. |
| Holiday week | Fri 25 Dec is a public holiday, and people are busy at year end. | Email 3 mentions the shop hours. Email 4 focuses corporate on getting quotations ready for the new year. |
