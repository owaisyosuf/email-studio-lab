# Campaign Plan: Send Us Your Size

**Business:** Baber Tyres Corporation, M.A. Jinnah Road, Karachi
**Dates:** Monday 7 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 (4 weeks)
**Channel:** Email only. No paid ads.
**Voice:** follows `brand/voice.md`

> Practice campaign. The list size, group sizes and targets below are made-up numbers for the Email Studio Lab. No real emails are sent.

**No website links.** The website's data is not complete yet, and it shows retail prices that are not right for corporate clients. So these emails do not mention or link to the website. Every email points to WhatsApp or a phone call. We can promote the website later in its own campaign, once it is complete.

---

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** Send Us Your Size
- **Summary:** Baber Tyres, a legal importer and dealer of genuine tyres, can now be reached directly on WhatsApp. Send us your tyre size (or your fleet's requirements) and we confirm everything with you.
- **Primary goal:** 300 WhatsApp enquiries by Sunday 3 January 2027. An enquiry is a car or SUV owner sending their tyre size, or a corporate client asking for a quotation.
- **Secondary goals:**
  - Customers learn which brands we import and which brands we deal in.
  - Car owners learn that we also offer fitting, computerized alignment and computerized balancing at the shop.
  - Corporate clients save our number as their tyre supplier for the new year.

## 2. Target audience

About 2,000 people are on our list, in five groups:

| # | Group | Practice list size | Who they are | What they need from us |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Car owners | 1,000 | Everyday drivers in Karachi | Guidance: how to read the size on their tyre, which brand fits, what happens after they message us. Many will want to talk price on WhatsApp. |
| 2 | SUV / 4x4 owners | 450 | SUV, 4x4 and off-road drivers | Help with larger and less common sizes, and the right tyre for road or off-road use. |
| 3 | Corporate: car fleets | 300 | Fleet and admin managers at multinationals, banks, beverage and logistics companies | A quick, clear way to get a quotation for many vehicles. |
| 4 | Corporate: trucks | 150 | Transport and logistics managers running trucks and commercial vehicles | Truck and commercial sizes (for example 11R22.5), and a quotation they can approve. |
| 5 | Corporate: lifters | 100 | Warehouse and operations managers running forklifts | Forklift sizes (for example 7.00-12), and a quotation they can approve. |

**Buying stage:** most people are at **consideration**. They know the name. The job of these emails is to move them to **decision**: send us your size or your requirements.

**Pain points and motivations:**
- **Car and SUV owners:** they don't know their exact size, worry about fake or used tyres, and don't want to visit several shops to compare.
- **Corporate:** buying for many vehicles takes time. They need a reliable supplier, genuine tyres and a quotation they can approve quickly.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** Send us your tyre size on WhatsApp, and we'll confirm the rest with you directly.

**Supporting messages:**

| Message | Proof point |
|---|---|
| **It's easy.** Your size is printed on the side of your tyre. Just send it to us. | Email 2 shows where to find it. |
| **Genuine and legally imported.** | We import Yokohama, Rapid, Michelin and Duhow ourselves, and we deal in Dunlop, General and Armstrong. We always tell you which is which, so you know who you are buying from. |
| **We confirm everything with you directly.** No guesswork. | We tell you what we have. If we don't have it, we say so. |
| **We can finish the job.** | Fitting, computerized alignment and computerized balancing at our shop on M.A. Jinnah Road. Delivery across Karachi is available, with charges confirmed on WhatsApp. |
| **Corporate: one supplier for your whole fleet.** | We already supply multinationals, banks, beverage and logistics companies, for cars, trucks and lifters. |

**Message and tone by group:**
- **Groups 1–2 (car and SUV owners):** friendly and guiding. Explain each step. Call to action: "Send us your tyre size on WhatsApp."
- **Groups 3–5 (corporate):** professional and short. Call to action: "Send us your sizes and quantities on WhatsApp, or call us, for a quotation." Never name a client.

**Never in any email:** website links, prices, discounts, "in stock", the word "warranty", or the words cheap, cheapest or sasta. Never blur imported and dealer brands.

## 4. Channel strategy

| Channel | Role | Why it fits | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Email** (the only outbound channel) | 4 emails, each with a version per group | The audience knows us, it costs nothing to send, and it fits a small budget. | Low–Medium |
| **WhatsApp and phone** (owned) | Where every email points, and where the goal is counted | It is how Baber Tyres already sells. | Medium: replies must be fast |
| **Website** | Not used in this campaign | Data not complete, and retail prices are not right for corporate clients. | – |

**Tracking WhatsApp replies:** each email uses its own pre-filled WhatsApp message, so we know which email brought each enquiry. For example:
- Car and SUV: `Hi Baber Tyres Corporation, my tyre size is ___ (email 1)`
- Corporate: `Hi Baber Tyres Corporation, we'd like a quotation for our fleet (email 1)`

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | What could happen | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| People don't know their tyre size | They don't message us at all. | Email 2 shows where the size is printed and what the numbers mean. Every email also says: "Not sure of your size? Message or call us and we'll guide you." |
| People search for us online and find the website | Corporate clients may see incomplete data or retail prices. | The emails never link to the website. Corporate replies get a proper quotation on WhatsApp. Finish the website before it gets its own campaign. |
| Too many WhatsApp messages at once | Slow replies after email 1 lose customers. | Send email 1 on Monday morning, use the reply templates, and have extra staff ready in week 1. |
| One message for very different groups | Car owners and forklift buyers need different things. | Write a version per group: guiding for car and SUV owners, quotation-focused for corporate. |
| Holiday week | Fri 25 Dec is a public holiday, and people are busy at year end. | Email 3 mentions the shop hours. Email 4 focuses corporate on getting quotations ready for the new year. |
