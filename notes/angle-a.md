# Angle A: a calm daily ritual

Subject line ideas for Email 1 ("Send us your size on WhatsApp").

The angle: your daily drive should feel calm and easy. Tyres you can trust are part of that routine, and getting them takes one WhatsApp message.

1. Your daily drive, made easy: send us your size
2. A calm start to every drive: send us your size
3. One WhatsApp message, and your drive feels easy

## Checks

- No prices, discounts, stock claims or "warranty".
- No cheap, cheapest or sasta.
- No website mention.
- Every line is 50 characters or fewer, counting the "1. " number.
- Every line points to sending the tyre size or a WhatsApp message.
- Best fit for car and SUV owners. Corporate groups would need a quotation-focused line instead.
