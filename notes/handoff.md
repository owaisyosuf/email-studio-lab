# Handoff: Email 1 subject line angle

- **Car owners:** easy guidance (most don't know their size) and trust (they worry about fake or used tyres).
- **SUV owners:** the right tyre for larger, less common sizes and for road or off-road use.
- **Corporate (fleet, trucks, lifters):** a quick, clear quotation they can approve, from a reliable supplier of genuine tyres.
- **Angle A (calm daily ritual):** your daily drive feels calm and easy, and one WhatsApp message gets you there.
- **Angle B (discover brands):** see your options across the 4 brands we import and the 3 we deal in. Send us your size.
- **Pick: Angle B.** Naming real brands answers the car and SUV owners' worry about fake tyres, and it invites them to ask for help choosing.
- Angle A is pleasant but vague. It doesn't speak to trust or to finding the right tyre.
- Neither angle fits corporate. Their Email 1 needs a quotation-focused subject line.
