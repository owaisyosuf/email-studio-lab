# Angle B: Discover brands (email 01)

The guide's angle is "discovering new teas". For Baber Tyres, the same angle is discovering tyre brands: the brands we import and the brands we deal in.

Three subject lines for email 01 (`campaigns/send-us-your-size/emails/01-send-us-your-size.md`):

1. Yokohama, Michelin or Dunlop? Send us your size
2. 7 tyre brands, one message: send us your size
3. The right brand for your car? Send us your size

## Voice checks

- No prices, discounts or "in stock".
- Line 1 names Yokohama and Michelin (brands we import) and Dunlop (a brand we deal in), so it does not call us the importer of any brand.
- Line 2 counts 7 brands: four we import and three we deal in.
- British spelling: "tyre".
- Every line is 50 characters or fewer, counting the "1. " number.
